# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students studying for the HSK (Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi) test, working independently to prepare for their exams.

## Product Purpose

To help students memorize vocabulary and prepare for the HSK test through a combination of flashcards, vocabulary lists, mock tests, and graded exercises.

## Positioning

A comprehensive HSK preparation tool that combines rote memorization aids (flashcards and vocabulary lists) with practical test application (mock tests and graded exercises) in a single platform.

## Operating Context

Students studying independently, likely in focused study sessions, requiring tools that support repeated practice and clear progress tracking.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Must strictly use standard HSK vocabulary lists and character sets.

## Brand Commitments

None confirmed.

## Evidence on Hand

None confirmed.

## Product Principles

- **Standardization over invention:** Stick strictly to official HSK vocabulary and characters.
- **Dual-mode learning:** Support both rote memorization (flashcards) and applied practice (mock tests).
- **Independent focus:** Design for self-directed study sessions.
